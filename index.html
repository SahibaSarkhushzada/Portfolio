Methodology
SEO & Content Framework
The 11-step process applied to every content brief — from keyword research to off-page authority building.
Phase 1 — Research
01
Audience & Intent Mapping

Define reader stage (awareness / consideration / decision) and what answer they need before writing a single word.

02
Competitor Gap Analysis

Use SEMrush to find keywords competitors rank for that you don't — low-hanging fruit and long-tail opportunities with conversion potential.

03
Keyword Clustering

Group keywords by topic and intent into content pillars mapped to specific page types to avoid cannibalization.

Phase 2 — On-Page
04
Title Tags & Meta

Primary keyword in title within 60 chars; compelling meta under 155 chars with value proposition and soft CTA.

05
Content Structure H1→H3

Logical heading hierarchy with keywords in H2/H3. Featured-snippet-ready: definition paragraphs, numbered lists, comparison tables.

06
Internal Linking

Link new content to existing high-authority pages, passing PageRank while improving time-on-site and crawl depth.

Phase 3 — Content Creation
07
E-E-A-T Principle

Every piece demonstrates Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust — essential for Google quality rater guidelines.

08
Semantic SEO & LSI

Latent Semantic Indexing keywords used naturally — helping Google understand the full topic context of every page.

09
6-Month Content Refresh

Audit existing content every 6 months — update stats, add sections, re-optimise for updated trends. Faster ROI than building from scratch.

Phase 4 — Off-Page & Reporting
10
Link Building

Guest posting on industry-relevant sites, digital PR and partner content exchanges to build domain authority sustainably.

11
Monthly KPI Dashboard

Track organic sessions, keyword positions, CTR, bounce rate and conversions in GSC + Analytics — actionable stakeholder reports.
